Visa adds Monad as a stablecoin settlement option

Visa added Monad as a stablecoin settlement option for participating issuers and acquirers, while a planned privacy feature for institutional payments remains in development.

The Finality DeskNov 11, 2026 · 04:37 UTC3 min read#727406

Visa has added the Monad blockchain as a stablecoin settlement option for participating issuers and acquirers, giving financial institutions another network for settling Visa obligations onchain. The Monad Foundation’s November 10 announcement says the option is available now; it does not identify participating institutions or the stablecoins they can use.

The change concerns settlement between payment institutions after card transactions. The Foundation says merchants and cardholders keep the familiar Visa experience while participating institutions can move settlement funds on Monad, including across weekends and holidays. It describes traditional card settlement as running on a five-business-day banking schedule.

What changes for Visa issuers and acquirers?

Participating issuers and acquirers gain another blockchain path for settling Visa obligations in stablecoins. The announcement says stablecoin settlement on Monad can reduce the operational friction of converting between stablecoins and fiat. It does not say that consumer card purchases themselves are now settled in stablecoins, or that all Visa partners can use the option.

Monad is an EVM-compatible layer 1. According to the Foundation, issuers and acquirers already using Ethereum-based custody, wallet and compliance tools can typically extend those integrations to Monad rather than build new ones. That compatibility may lower integration work, but the release does not name any institution that has completed an integration.

The Foundation also cites sub-second finality as a basis for settling transactions moments after submission and giving acquirers more predictable settlement cycles. It lists 800-millisecond finality and 400-millisecond block times for the network. Those are Monad network figures, not a disclosed end-to-end Visa settlement time.

Which settlement assets and partners are confirmed?

The announcement confirms the network option, but does not specify which stablecoins Visa partners may settle on Monad or name any issuers or acquirers using it. Unchained’s report on the announcement also notes that Visa had not published a separate announcement as of its publication. The available information therefore establishes an expanded option for participating partners, not evidence of a particular asset, bank or settlement volume on Monad.

The release puts Visa’s stablecoin settlement run rate at $25 billion annualized as of September 2026, roughly 10 times the level a year earlier, with more than 225 stablecoin-linked card programs live globally. Those are Visa-wide figures cited by Monad, not activity attributed to this new network option.

When will Monad’s privacy feature be available?

Not yet. The Foundation says its Private Settlement functionality is in development and is expected to reach Visa partners in the coming months. It is designed to keep commercially sensitive settlement details confidential while allowing institutions to share them with auditors and regulators.

The Foundation says the feature will run directly on Monad, keeping private activity connected to the network’s shared liquidity. It also says privacy is designed not to come at the cost of settlement speed. The announcement does not provide a launch date or technical specification for how transaction data will be restricted or disclosed. Until that feature is available, the announced addition gives partners another settlement network, while the confidentiality capability remains a future part of the proposal.
