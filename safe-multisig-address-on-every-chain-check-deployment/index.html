Safe multisig address on every chain: check deployment

Does a Safe multisig have the same address on every chain? Only when deployed with matching factory, setup data and salt; confirm owners before bridging USDC.

The Finality DeskNov 28, 2026 · 04:37 UTC4 min read#2d28a5

A Safe multisig wallet gives a team a shared on-chain account, and it has the same address on another chain only when that chain deploys it with the same SafeProxyFactory, singleton, setup data and salt. SafeProxyFactory uses CREATE2 to compute the address from those inputs, not from the owners’ keys.

Does a Safe multisig have the same address on every chain?

A Safe multisig can have the same address on multiple EVM chains, but the matching address must be derived from matching deployment inputs. The factory address, singleton, initializer and saltNonce determine the proxy address through CREATE2. The initializer contains the Safe’s initial setup, including its owners and threshold.

That makes the address predictable before deployment. It does not make the Safe live on every chain where the address can be calculated. The destination chain needs the corresponding factory and singleton, and the Safe must be deployed there before its owners can use it.

A USDC bridge transfer adds a recipient check: the address must be ready to control funds on the destination chain. For the recurring-payment decision, read our article on choosing a USDC bridge for repeat payments.

When does the same Safe address exist on another chain?

The same Safe address exists when the destination chain can reproduce the CREATE2 deployment using the same factory, singleton, initializer and saltNonce. Most EVM chains can reproduce this setup when the required Safe contracts are available at the corresponding addresses. Chains with different address derivation, such as zkSync Era, cannot produce the same address through this process.

Consider two otherwise identical destination chains with the same Safe deployment inputs. On the first, the SafeProxyFactory has deployed the proxy, so the Safe is live at the computed address. On the second, the inputs still compute that same address, but no proxy has been deployed there. The deciding factor is deployment: only the first address is a usable Safe.

Before treating an address as ready, confirm the network in Safe{Wallet}, check that the Safe is deployed there, and verify the displayed owners and threshold. A matching string of characters alone does not establish that the destination contract exists.

What happens if USDC reaches an undeployed Safe address?

USDC sent to an undeployed address sits there until someone deploys that exact Safe on the destination chain. The token transfer can reach the address even though the Safe contract is not yet present to manage the funds.

In 2022, Wintermute lost 20 million OP sent to its Safe address on Optimism, where that Safe had not been deployed. The incident shows why a team must check destination deployment before sending a large cross-chain amount.

Deployment must recreate the intended Safe at that address. If the factory, singleton, initializer or saltNonce differs, the deployed Safe can have a different address, leaving the tokens at the original address. Confirm the deployment details before relying on a recovery plan.

Do Safe owners need to sign again on each chain?

Safe owners must sign transactions for the Safe on each chain where the team wants to act. Safe transaction signatures include the chain ID, so a signature for one chain cannot be replayed on another.

Replicating a Safe reuses its original setup data. The new chain’s Safe therefore starts with the original owner set and threshold. If the team changed owners or its threshold later on the first chain, those changes must also be made on the new chain before its governance matches.

Split the setup work clearly. One member adds the network in Safe{Wallet} and arranges deployment; any wallet can submit and pay for the deployment transaction. The signers confirm the owner list and threshold on that chain. The treasurer then sends a small USDC bridge test and checks receipt before moving the full amount.

Can a Safe receive USDC on Solana?

A Safe cannot receive USDC on Solana as a Safe account, because Safe is an EVM smart contract wallet and Solana uses a different address and account system. A bridge can deliver USDC to a Solana address, but the team needs a Solana-compatible recipient it controls for that transfer.

Once the destination Safe is deployed, its initial owners and threshold are verified, and the test transfer arrives, the team has a concrete basis for sending the remaining funds. Confirm those steps together before authorizing the full bridge amount.
