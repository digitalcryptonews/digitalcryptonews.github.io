Why Did My Supplied WETH Disappear From MetaMask?

Why are supplied lending tokens missing from MetaMask? After a SparkLend supply, WETH moves to the reserve and spWETH represents your position.

The Finality DeskNov 19, 2026 · 13:58 UTC5 min read#dfef09

After a completed supply, your WETH leaves your MetaMask wallet because it has moved into the lending reserve; spWETH represents your supplied position. MetaMask may not show that receipt token in its wallet list, so the underlying balance can fall while the supplied value remains represented onchain.

How does a supply change the wallet balance?

A supply exchanges the wallet’s underlying asset for a claim represented by a receipt token. In SparkLend, supplied assets go into a smart contract, and spTokens reflect the supplier’s position. The wallet’s WETH and the position’s spWETH are different assets with different roles.

Say you supply 1 WETH. After the transaction succeeds, your wallet’s WETH balance is lower by 1 WETH, while the lending position and its spWETH balance represent the supply. The transaction has moved the underlying WETH into the reserve; it has not simply hidden the same WETH from MetaMask.

This distinction matters whenever you compare a wallet balance with a lending position. A wallet token list shows assets associated with the account that MetaMask is currently displaying. A supplied position records the claim received for assets committed to lending. Those views can therefore show different tokens and amounts without contradicting each other.

Why can MetaMask hide spWETH?

MetaMask can omit spWETH because token detection and token display depend on which assets its wallet list identifies. The receipt token can exist at the account’s address onchain even when it is absent from the visible list.

To reconcile the displays, confirm that MetaMask is showing the same account and network used for the supply. Then identify the official spWETH contract address for that network and compare it with the receipt token address. A token with a similar name or ticker is not enough to establish that it is the correct receipt token.

MetaMask supports adding a custom token by contract address. Importing the verified spWETH address makes MetaMask look up and display the token balance already associated with the account. This changes the wallet’s local display; it does not send a blockchain transaction, require an onchain approval, or consume gas.

That makes a verified import useful when you need to see the receipt token alongside the rest of your wallet assets. It does not move WETH back into the wallet. The position remains supplied until you withdraw it through the lending protocol.

How can I tell a missing display from a failed supply?

Compare the supply transaction, the receipt token balance, and the supplied position. A successful transaction on Etherscan, checked for the correct account and network, should correspond to the position created by the supply. The receipt token balance is another way to find the claim if MetaMask did not list it automatically.

For the 1 WETH case, look for the successful supply transaction from your account, then confirm that the matching position and spWETH balance exist. If MetaMask shows no spWETH, adding the official contract address can reveal that existing balance. The local wallet display and the onchain position are separate records of what you hold and where it is held.

If the account has no corresponding receipt balance and no supplied position, the display explanation does not account for the missing value. Investigate the transaction itself on Etherscan: confirm its status, the sending account, the destination contract, and the asset and amount involved. A different account or network can also make the expected receipt balance appear absent from the wallet view.

For repeat checks, write down the account, network, and official receipt contract address used for the supply before comparing balances. That small record helps you match the right onchain position and avoids mistaking an omitted token from another account or network for a failed supply.

What should I know next?

How do I withdraw the underlying tokens?

Withdraw through the lending protocol to redeem the supplied position for the underlying asset. The withdrawal uses the receipt-token claim, and available liquidity and any borrowing collateral requirements can affect whether the assets can be withdrawn at that time.

Can I transfer spTokens while borrowing?

Transferring spTokens transfers control of the represented supply claim and can change the collateral supporting a borrow. Review the resulting position and its collateral before transferring, since reducing supplied collateral can affect the borrowing position.

How does interest change my spToken balance?

Interest changes the value of your supplied position as borrowers pay interest into the reserve. The position’s accounting reflects that yield; compare the position value and receipt-token balance using the protocol’s accounting, since a displayed wallet amount alone may not communicate the accrued value.

Why does MetaMask show no dollar value for spTokens?

MetaMask may display a token balance without showing a dollar estimate when it does not have a price for that receipt token. The missing estimate concerns price display; use the token balance and the supplied position to reconcile the onchain claim.
