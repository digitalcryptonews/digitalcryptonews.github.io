About Finality

Finality reports on crypto as a technical beat: protocol upgrades, consensus and client releases, exchange and custody incidents, stablecoins, and the rules being written around them. We cover many projects across the industry and are not affiliated with any of them.

Stories are built from primary sources such as code repositories, governance proposals, on-chain records, filings and official statements, and each one names the mechanism behind the event. When we get something wrong, we correct the article and note the change. Finality is independent, and nothing published here is financial advice.
